Privacy Policy

Hasta+: Hand Gesture Control · Effective 3 November 2026

Hasta+ reads your hand gestures with the front camera, entirely on your phone. Camera frames are processed in memory and thrown away; they are never saved, uploaded or used for training. There are no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only internet traffic is for Google Play and, for free users, ads from Google AdMob.

What we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. Hasta+ has no sign-in and no servers of its own, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. The ad and Google Play services described below are run by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

The camera

  • On the phone only. Hand tracking uses an open-source on-device model (Google MediaPipe), with Hasta AI, our own recognition layer, running on top of it. Both work fully offline.
  • Frames are never kept. Each camera frame is turned into hand-position numbers (landmarks) in memory and then discarded. No frame is written to storage or sent anywhere.
  • Only while it's on. The camera runs only while gesture control is turned on, so Android's camera indicator always tells the truth. It pauses on the lock screen unless you allow it.

What stays on your phone

Hasta+ keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your settings, gesture mappings, macros and which apps use which gestures
  • Custom gestures you train, stored as hand-position numbers, never as images
  • Daily counts of which gestures you used, and a short log of when gesture control started, stopped or paused (shown on Home)
  • Game scores, streaks and daily lives for the Hasta+ widget games

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Hasta+, so none of this is copied to Google or any other cloud.

Photos, videos and drawings you save

When you take a photo or video with Hasta+ Camera, save a Magic Pad drawing, or save a game score card to share, the file is saved to your phone's gallery (Pictures/Hasta+ or Movies/Hasta+). Like any gallery file, other apps you have given media access can see it. We never receive a copy.

Backups you export

Settings › Backup can save your settings, gesture mappings, macros and custom gestures (as numbers, never images) to a file wherever you choose, and restore them later. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. We never receive a copy.

Accessibility service

If you turn it on, Hasta+ uses Android's Accessibility Service API to carry out the actions you assign to gestures, such as scrolling, going back, or pressing a button like Like or Next. To press a named button, it looks at the buttons on the screen at that moment to find the right one. It does not store, log or send what is on your screen, and it is blocked on password fields and banking apps by default. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings › Accessibility.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Camera: to see your hand gestures, and to take photos and videos in Hasta+ Camera.
  • Microphone: only to record sound with videos in Hasta+ Camera. You can turn sound off.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show that gesture control is running and let you stop it.
  • Run at startup: to bring gesture control back after a restart, if you chose that.
  • Media controls (optional notification access): only to control what's playing, such as seeking or liking. Hasta+ does not read your notifications.
  • Phone calls (optional): asked for only if you assign a gesture to answer or reject calls.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on Privacy lock. Android does the check; Hasta+ never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Internet: only for ads and Google Play, described below.

Hasta+ does not use your location or contacts.

Ads

Hasta+ shows no ads during the 3-day free preview or with a paid plan. After the preview, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. In the Hasta+ widget games, you can also choose to watch a short video ad to get an extra life. Like most mobile ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Play services

Hasta+ asks the Google Play Store app whether a newer version is available so it can ask you to update, and the Rate Hasta+ button opens the app's Google Play page. These are handled by Google Play under your Google account; Hasta+ sends none of your data to Google.

Purchases

Paid plans are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Hasta+ checks with Google Play only which plan you own.

Deleting your data

You can remove custom gestures, mappings and macros inside the app. Uninstalling Hasta+ permanently deletes everything it stored in its private storage. Photos, videos and drawings in your gallery are separate files that you can delete yourself.

Children

Hasta+ is not directed at children under 13. We collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
