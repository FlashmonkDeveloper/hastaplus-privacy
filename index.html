📋What we collect

Hasta+ works without an account, and you never give us your name, email or phone number. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. The only information that leaves your phone is:

  • for the Invite friends programme, a small anonymous record described below, stored in Google Firebase;
  • what Google needs to show ads to free users (Ads) and to run Google Play (Google Play and purchases), handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

📷The camera

  • On the phone only. Hand tracking uses an open-source on-device model (Google MediaPipe), with Hasta AI, our own recognition layer, running on top of it. Both work fully offline.
  • Frames are never kept. Each camera frame is turned into hand-position numbers (landmarks) in memory and then discarded. No frame is written to storage or sent anywhere.
  • Only while it's on. The camera runs only while gesture control is turned on, so Android's camera indicator always tells the truth. It pauses on the lock screen unless you allow it.

📱What stays on your phone

Hasta+ keeps the following in its private storage on your device, and nowhere else:

  • Your settings, gesture mappings, macros and which apps use which gestures
  • Custom gestures you train, stored as hand-position numbers, never as images
  • Daily counts of which gestures you used, and a short log of when gesture control started, stopped or paused
  • Game scores, streaks and daily lives for the Hasta+ widget games

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Hasta+. Backups you export (Settings › Privacy and backup) are saved as a file wherever you choose; the file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Photos, videos and drawings you save from Hasta+ Camera, Magic Pad or the games go to your gallery (Pictures/Hasta+ or Movies/Hasta+), where other apps with media access can see them. We never receive a copy of any of these.

🎁Invite friends New

You can share a link that gives a friend a longer free preview, and earns you a month of premium for each friend who keeps using Hasta+. To count invites fairly and stop abuse, Hasta+ uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions and Cloud Firestore, run by Google) and the Google Play Integrity API.

The server keeps, for each phone: its invite code, who invited it, those days, how many friends joined and qualified, and how much premium was earned. Nothing else: no camera images, contacts, location, screen content or anything you type. The data is stored in Google Cloud in Mumbai, India, is only reachable by our server functions, and is never shared or used for ads. It is kept while invites are in use; to delete it, email us your invite code (Settings › Invite friends) and we will remove it within 30 days.

♿Accessibility service

If you turn it on, Hasta+ uses Android's Accessibility Service API to carry out the actions you assign to gestures, such as scrolling, going back, or pressing a button like Like or Next. To press a named button, it looks at the buttons on the screen at that moment to find the right one. It does not store, log or send what is on your screen, and it is blocked on password fields and banking apps by default. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings › Accessibility.

🔑Permissions and why they are used

  • Camera: to see your hand gestures, and to take photos and videos in Hasta+ Camera.
  • Microphone: only to record sound with videos in Hasta+ Camera. You can turn sound off.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show that gesture control is running and let you stop it, and to tell you when a friend you invited qualifies.
  • Run at startup: to bring gesture control back after a restart, if you chose that.
  • Media controls (optional notification access): only to control what's playing, such as seeking or liking. Hasta+ does not read your notifications.
  • Phone calls (optional): asked for only if you assign a gesture to answer or reject calls.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on Privacy lock. Android does the check; Hasta+ never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Internet: for ads, Google Play and Invite friends, as described here.
  • Installed apps (WhatsApp): only to show a WhatsApp share button when WhatsApp is installed.

Hasta+ does not use your location or contacts.

📣Ads

Hasta+ shows no ads during the free preview (3 days, or 7 when a friend invited you) or with premium. After the preview, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and in the widget games you can choose to watch a short video for an extra life. Like most mobile ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings › Privacy and backup. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

🛒Google Play and purchases

Hasta+ asks the Google Play Store app whether a newer version is available, and the Rate Hasta+ button opens the app's Google Play page. Paid plans are bought through Google Play: Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. Hasta+ checks with Google Play only which plan you own.

🗑️Deleting your data

You can remove custom gestures, mappings and macros inside the app. Uninstalling Hasta+ permanently deletes everything it stored on your phone. Photos, videos and drawings in your gallery are separate files you can delete yourself. For your Invite friends record, email us your invite code and we will delete it within 30 days.

✉️Children, changes and contact

Children. Hasta+ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes. If a future version changes how data is handled, this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes.
